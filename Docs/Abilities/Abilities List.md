# Q Abilities
## Beam

The player charges (short time) a beam that deals damage on contact.
The player can't move during charge time.

Long Range.
Big Damage.
Long cooldown.

(like a smaller Lux R or a static but fast Xerath Q)

## Magic Missile

The player launches 3 consecutive missiles that deal damage on impact.
The missiles target the players mouse position at cast.
There's a short pause between casts.
The player can move while the cast, but at a slower speed.
The travel time/impact speed of each missile increases with distance from the player.

Medium Range.
Small (Individual) Damage
Medium Cooldown

(small, automatic Xerath R or a 3 step Mel Q)
![[Pasted image 20260815183250.png|286]]
![[Pasted image 20260815183230.png]]

## Shot

The player shoots a fast moving projective in a straight line that deals contact damage with a small explosion on end of travel.

Medium Range.
Medium Damage
Medium Cooldown


(Hwei Q)
![[Pasted image 20260815183922.png|316]]

## Shotgun

The player shoots a 5 projectiles that spread apart on travel, in a cone area.
This ability can hold 2 charges.

Small Range
Small (Individual) Damage
Medium Cooldown

(Smaller Ashe W or Longer Graves Auto)
