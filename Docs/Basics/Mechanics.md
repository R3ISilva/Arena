# Movement
Mouse based movement
Right click to pick a spot, the character moves there automatically with pathfinding.


# Combat

## Attack
No Auto Attack for now.

## Abilities
Each player has 3 abilities (Spells) bound to the Q,W and E keys.

#### Burst Abilities
Focused on Burst Damage and skill shots.
Faster Cooldowns

#### Area Abilities
Focused on AOE Damage, Area Denial and CC
Medium Cooldowns

#### Extra Abilities
Focused on Buffs, Debuffs and Movement
Long Cooldowns

### Ability Selection
Before a match starts the players take turns picking abilities.
For each spell slot there's a list of abilities the players can pick from.
The players pick the abilities in order (Q, W, E), alternating between who gets to pick first.
Player's can't pick the same ability.
