# Dirt Tiles
9 Tiles
These tiles fill every bit of floor
Can be rotated (0º 90º 180º 270º)

## Mapping
* Tiles 0-8: Dirt Tile
Can be placed in any way/order


# Stone Tiles
9 Tiles (8 Filled)
These tiles only fill partial areas of the floor
Ideally fuller tiles are surrounded by more broken tiles before reaching empty tiles
Can't be flipped or rotated

## Mapping
* Tiles 0-2: Full Stone Tile
* Tiles 3-5: Broken Stone Tile
* Tiles 6-7: Stone Shards Tile
* Tiles 8: No Stone Tile
Tiles change gradually
Tiles 0-2 are rare
Tiles 8 are the most common
Avoid having tiles 0-2 touch Tiles 8
